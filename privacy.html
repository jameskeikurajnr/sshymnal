Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: S-SHymnal does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your hymns, notes, highlights, favourites, services, MP3s, pinned videos and Bibles are kept only on your own device.
  • If you use iCloud or device backups, your device may include the app's files in them. That is handled by Apple, not by me.
  • The morning notification is created on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Services provided by others

  • Speak to search: uses your device's speech recognition. Apple may process your speech to turn it into text, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Watch: opens a YouTube search or plays a video you chose to pin. YouTube then follows Google's own privacy policy.
  • Word help: if you ask for a word to be explained, only that word is requested from a free public dictionary service (dictionaryapi.dev).
  • Pinned videos: when you pin a YouTube video, the app asks YouTube for the video's title. Only the video link is sent.
  • These services can see your device's internet address, as any website does. The app sends them nothing else and does not send them your name or any account details.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
