Support

If something looks wrong

  1. Close the app and open it again.
  2. If a bar at the top says safe mode, a feature failed last time. Use the app normally and it returns to full mode.
  3. Open Settings, then Backup & Safety, and save a backup before you change anything big.
  4. No sound? Check your volume and the silent switch.
  5. Microphone not working? Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for S-SHymnal in your device settings.
